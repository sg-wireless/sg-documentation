Your First Sensor Data
======================

.. TODO:: Migrate content from https://docs.sgwireless.com/getting-started/your-first-sensor-data/

This guide walks you through connecting an SGW8130 CAP/T BLE sensor to your F1
Starter Kit and sending temperature and humidity readings to the Ctrl. Cloud.

Prerequisites
-------------

* Provisioned F1 Starter Kit (see :doc:`ztp` or :doc:`manual-provisioning`)
* SGW8130 CAP/T BLE sensor
