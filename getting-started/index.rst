Getting Started
===============

We designed the F1 Starter Kit to get you connected straight out of the box.

Begin your IoT journey in three simple steps:

1. :doc:`Provision your F1 Starter Kit <ztp>` -- connect it to Ctrl through your
   preferred network connection.
2. :doc:`Set up your CAP/T sensor <first-sensor-data>` -- send its data to Ctrl
   through F1.
3. :doc:`Execute your first code <first-f1-code>` -- get your first "Hello F1"
   printed out.
