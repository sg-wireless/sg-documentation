Taking Ctrl.
============

The all-in-one Cloud Platform that lets you configure, deploy and manage your
devices to give you full Ctrl. over your IoT networks.

* Visualize your sensor data
* Check the status of your entire deployment
* Distribute firmware updates on a scalable approach

In a nutshell, Ctrl is an environment designed to optimize your IoT experience
when using F1 smart modules.
